## Reproduction

From this device folder, run:

    python3 tools/verify.py

The check requires gcc, arm-none-eabi-gcc/binutils, Python with Tk, Xvfb, KiCad CLI and the system Python pcbnew binding. No package installation or live device is used by the automated checks. The generated serial fixture is synthetic and lives in ignored firmware/build/; it is generated by the production C encoder during the test, not presented as measured catch data.

## Explicitly not verified

No PCB was fabricated or assembled. No MCU was flashed. No electrical rail, ADC linearity, oscillator accuracy, serial behavior on physical USB hardware, ESD immunity or regulator margin was measured. The purchased Nucleo and external sensors are outside the passive carrier ERC boundary.

No mechanical fixture was fabricated. Spring stiffness, material condition, clamp compliance, friction, overload stops, zero repeatability, temperature sensitivity and fixture-inclusive work have not been measured. The force scale is nominal. In particular, the sliding potentiometer's operating force can dominate small catch differences; successful software tests do not resolve that risk. A reference force gauge and a bidirectional blank-fixture characterization are required before quantitative use.

The design is differentiated against the inventoried repository and includes closely related external prior art. Worldwide novelty, patentability, supplier availability, price quotations and commercial utility were not established. This was a standalone-owner implementation with advisory reports, not independent collective sign-off.
