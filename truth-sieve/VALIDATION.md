- C relation test: passed with address and undefined-behavior sanitizers. All 65536 retained settings sets were compared against enumeration of 256 deterministic maps each. All sixteen raw words were checked for next-input guidance at every retained set. Every ordered stable-word pair was exercised through acquisition. Threshold, gap, persistent conflict, wrap, terminal cause, overflow rollback, small buffer and CRC regressions passed.
- Production clock/configuration and UART driver tests: passed with simulated register storage. They cover bounded clock failure, input configuration, receive-disabled contract, serial busy/length rejection, byte draining, timer wrap and timeout recovery. They are not physical register-access tests.
- `xvfb-run -a /usr/bin/python3 -m unittest discover -s truth-sieve/tests -p 'test_*.py'`: 10 tests passed under Python 3.13.5. Includes exhaustive host relation checks, real C-encoder fixture replay, CRC-valid impossible records, fragment recovery, strict EOF handling, special-file refusal, pseudo-terminal serial attributes, actual Tk screens, private export, overwrite refusal, live stale handling, reconnect clearing and failed-open preservation.

- `/usr/bin/python3 truth-sieve/tools/check_hardware.py`: passed using KiCad CLI 9.0.2. Native ERC: zero violations. Native DRC: zero violations and zero unconnected items. A freshly exported schematic XML netlist matched all 30 PCB/manifest pins, values and full footprint identifiers across the 14 components and 10 nets.

## Limitations and remaining acceptance

No fabricated PCB, flashed MCU, real USB serial instrument, physical switch fixture, calibrated timing reference, electrical measurement or user experiment was available. The synthetic half-adder fixture is generated by firmware logic and explicitly labelled synthetic in its source; it is not measured device output. CRC does not authenticate traffic. Sparse transition checks constrain necessary accounting bounds, not every possible hidden contact history. Without persistent boot identity some resets cannot be inferred reliably.

The custom board is a passive carrier for a separately purchased NUCLEO-F401RE. Passive-only ERC cannot verify the external regulator, bridge configuration, power demand or GPIO thresholds. Mechanical contact force, contact wetting, bounce, cable noise, enclosure fit, polarity protection, ESD behavior and real settling behavior remain to be assessed. A stable four-bit observation is not causal proof, a safety certificate or a guarantee of the intended logic function. See the README for physical bring-up and proposed acceptance experiments.
