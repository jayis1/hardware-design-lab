## Review corrections

Read-only review identified that the final rising endpoint was omitted from falling-to-rising rearm accounting, making an advertised twenty-millisecond boundary effectively twenty-one. A regression first reproduced the failure; firmware now accepts exactly twenty and rejects nineteen. Sparse companion validation also accepted a HIGH interval whose elapsed duration did not advance, and a complete pulse impossible in the available time. Regressions reproduced these before adding state-aware minimum-time and held-payload checks. All tests above passed after these corrections.

## Unverified and intentionally limited

No board fabrication, soldering, programming, physical contact acquisition, mechanical-strip experiment, calibrated timing, noise/ESD qualification or product usability testing has occurred. The passive carrier checks cannot validate external power, the purchased development-board revision, switch minimum load, contact bounce or harness orientation. Mechanical fixture dimensions are design starting points, not supplied enclosure/fixture manufacturing CAD. Strict raw-clock rejection may produce nuisance halts with real switches; characterization is required.

There is no persistent reference, boot nonce, independently sensed slot identity or direction indication. Undetected missing physical positions and some MCU resets remain possible. Latest slot values are not necessarily from one complete repeat. Fresh serial traffic is not proof of valid acquisition, and consistency checks are not a complete proof of physical history. Prior-art searching was bounded; no worldwide novelty or patent claim is made.
