Fault injection verifies terminal restoration when serial setup fails after changing attributes.

## Corrected findings and regression evidence

Tests first reproduced baseline recovery failure after an aborted trial, incomplete recovery when timing out while already at baseline, and acceptance of a checksummed impossible replay record. The fixes reevaluate qualified baseline without requiring an extra edge and validate completion flags, counters, ordering and bounded event span. A further negative fixture with individually legal offsets spanning eight seconds is now rejected against the five-second capture limit.

The timing engine now qualifies each contact independently. A regression opens A, changes an unrelated pedal input seven milliseconds later, then presses B thirty milliseconds after A. The measured key gap remains thirty milliseconds rather than being shortened by a shared debounce timer. Other exercised cases include early pedal release, key overlap, simultaneous observations, endpoint contradiction, timeout, acquisition gaps, B release, A reattack, short rejected pulses, ordinary timer wrap, saturating diagnostics, CRC reference vector and undersized output buffers.

Two read-only review passes were used. Reported correctness defects were addressed locally, with failing tests observed before the corresponding fixes. The sole implementation author is jayis1.

## Evidence boundaries

No PCB was fabricated; no target was flashed; no piano was instrumented. MCU clock tolerance, physical contact hysteresis, mechanical mounting, actual USB current, input voltage margins under noise, electrical transients, ESD immunity and usefulness to teachers remain unmeasured. Firmware cross-linking proves executable construction, not electrical operation. PTY tests simulate transport and contact traces rather than demonstrating actual ST-LINK USB behavior.

The passive carrier depends on a separately purchased NUCLEO-F401RE. Its ERC cannot validate that board's supply or solder-bridge configuration. The pin map and virtual-COM arrangement were checked against the available UM1724 manual text and indexed manufacturer documentation. Direct manufacturer PDF fetching failed in this environment; existing local manual text and retrieved manufacturer excerpts provided the connector evidence. No failed download was treated as a successful measurement or a new source file.

The power budget and component prices in the README are explicitly estimates. Current and divider-voltage calculations were executed locally. The prior-art search found existing piano-pedaling measurement research, so the documentation does not claim global first invention or patent novelty.

A physical bring-up, mechanical fixture review and supervised teaching evaluation are the next engineering gates before relying on this prototype in a lesson. There are no remaining failed software or CAD release gates in this revision.
